# VISION.md — Long-Term Vision

## Mission

Make academic research discovery effortless, intelligent, and accessible to everyone.

## Vision Statement

> By 2027, ResearchPilot will be the default starting point for any research endeavor —
> a conversational assistant that understands your research goals, maintains context across
> sessions, and proactively surfaces the papers that matter most to your work.

## Phase 1: Paper Finder (Current)
- Topic-based paper search
- AI-evaluated relevance scoring
- Structured results with abstracts and links

## Phase 2: Research Assistant
- Multi-turn conversations about papers
- Literature review generation
- Citation graph visualization
- Reading list management

## Phase 3: Knowledge Engine
- Personal paper library with embeddings
- Semantic search across saved papers
- Auto-summarization of paper collections
- Gap analysis: "What's missing from my review?"

## Phase 4: Collaboration Platform
- Shared research spaces
- Team annotations and notes
- Paper discussion threads
- Integration with Notion, Obsidian, Zotero

## Success Metrics

| Metric | Phase 1 Target | Phase 3 Target |
|--------|----------------|----------------|
| Daily active users | 100 | 10,000 |
| Papers returned per search | 5-10 | 20-50 |
| User satisfaction (CSAT) | 4.0/5 | 4.5/5 |
| Time to find relevant paper | <30s | <10s |
